## Three-paper route

1. **Paper one:** verify publication identity/version; preserve authorized original bytes with a computed SHA256 or record a qualified external pointer. Create Source and Literature drafts. Every Source needs the content contract below.
2. **Paper two:** repeat, then create one justified Topic comparing the two studies' questions, mechanisms, sample/period, estimands, identification assumptions, findings, limitations and overlap. Reuse an existing Topic where appropriate. Versions of one study and shared data are not independent corroboration.
3. **Paper three:** create its Source/Literature drafts, then propose a substantive revision to that SAME Topic, not just another citation. Show old wording → proposed wording → exact source/version/locator → rationale and applicability. Stop for authorization before established content changes. Default maximum existing research notes edited: **one Topic**, with index/log as additional bookkeeping. If evidence warrants no change, record the reason honestly instead of manufacturing a finding; do not call that a demonstrated substantive update.

## Source content contract

Every enrolled Source needs a readable, source-grounded summary with:

- **Research question(s)** and why the paper matters to the chosen scope.
- **Mechanism/theory:** reported channel, assumptions or propositions; distinguish author interpretation from established evidence.
- **Data/sample/period:** setting, units, coverage, dataset provider/version/vintage where available. For theory-only work, say not applicable.
- **Identification:** the actual reported strategy, assumptions and threats; explicitly absent, not assessed or not applicable when appropriate. Never invent causal identification or convert descriptive associations into causal findings.
- **Estimand:** reported target quantity, treatment/comparison/outcome or theoretical object; not applicable/unknown with explanation when justified.
- **Findings:** reported result, units, specification and uncertainty where available; do not calculate or fabricate missing values.
- **Limits and applicability:** external validity, data/inference limitations, disagreement and relevance.
- **Evidence scope and locators:** what was actually read, short exact supporting quotes, page/table/column/section/proposition or packet page/line locators, verified identity/version and original link. Abstract-only notes remain partial; acquisition is not completed paraphrase.

JEL codes are verified metadata, not guessed classifications. Preserve working-paper and journal versions, shared samples, dataset vintage/classification/units/licensing and distinct policy announcement/adoption/effective/amendment dates. Separate descriptive, causal, theoretical, counterfactual and independently reproduced evidence. Only actual data/code/environment/execution/output records justify newly computed results or replication.

This is a **content completion contract**. Existing economics intake does not require a `--content-evidence` flag, and lint does not enforce all narrative fields or validate causal identification. Do not import a medical/PubMed acquisition gate. Intake's packet/proposal validator checks quote/locator provenance mechanically; it does not perform economist appraisal or revise existing Topics.

## Version and human-review boundary

Before a material edit preserve the old note bytes and its bounded human decision/receipt in a private, non-indexed version archive outside compiled research folders (or the user's approved versioned backup). Record old version/hash, reviewer/date/scope/decision, proposed change and affected paths in the review ledger/log. Use actual tool-computed hashes, not placeholders.

Set materially changed current content to `review_status: economist-review-needed`. Remove current approval fields (`reviewer`, `reviewed_date`, `review_scope`, `review_decision`) from that changed note after archiving them; never delete historical decisions, re-date an approval or re-pin a receipt to new text. Unchanged approved versions retain their historical status. A fresh human expert must authorize bounded new approval. Review receipts detect changed bytes; they neither authenticate a reviewer nor prove econometric validity. `review-content.cjs` writes a human-confirmed receipt; it does not itself promote note frontmatter.
